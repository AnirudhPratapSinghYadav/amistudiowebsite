# Brand voice and copy

## Voice
- Direct, calm confidence. An engineer-producer talking to an artist across the desk.
- Hook first: the first line of every section states what the artist gets or what happens next.
- Plain words. Short sentences. One idea per line.

## Hard rules
1. Source every claim from /content/facts.md. If it is not there, do not write it.
2. No emojis. No em dashes. No exclamation marks except where /content/copy.md already has one.
3. Banned words and phrases: elevate, unleash, seamless, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, world-class, next-level, journey, passion, game-changer, revolutionary, "in today's fast-paced", "whether you are".
4. No "we have worked with [names]" or any social proof unless provided with written permission [CONFIRM].
5. Prices only as stated: vocal recording $40 per hour, production quoted per song and genre.
6. Buttons are verbs: Book a session, Send the details, See the room.

## Hook patterns (use, do not repeat the same one twice on a page)
- Outcome: "Walk in with an idea. Walk out with a record."
- Removal of fear: "No reference beat? No problem."
- Specific action: "Tell us the genre. We will tell you what it takes."

## Process
1. Draft from copy.md. 2. Check each sentence against facts.md. 3. Strip banned words. 4. Read aloud test: if it sounds like an ad agency wrote it, rewrite it shorter.
